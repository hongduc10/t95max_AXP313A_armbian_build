===== 34_ethernet_driver =====
Command: for d in /sys/class/net/*; do echo ==== $d ====; readlink -f $d/device/driver; readlink -f $d/phydev; cat $d/address 2>/dev/null; cat $d/speed 2>/dev/null; cat $d/duplex 2>/dev/null; done
