===== 36_sdio_devices =====
Command: for d in /sys/bus/sdio/devices/*; do [ -e "$d" ] || continue; echo ==== $d ====; for f in vendor device class modalias uevent; do echo --- $f; cat $d/$f 2>/dev/null; done; readlink -f $d/driver; done
